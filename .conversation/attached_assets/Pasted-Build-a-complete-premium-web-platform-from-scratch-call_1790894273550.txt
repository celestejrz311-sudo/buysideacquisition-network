Build a complete premium web platform from scratch called BuySide.
BuySide is a global private business acquisition marketplace.
The core idea is simple:
Buyers publish exactly what type of business they want to acquire. Brokers, business owners, M&A advisors, and deal finders can browse those buyer requests, find matching businesses, and submit opportunities. If a submitted opportunity leads to a completed acquisition, the finder may earn a success-based reward.
This is NOT a traditional business-for-sale marketplace.
The platform should be centered around buyer demand first.
Instead of waiting for a business to be listed for sale, buyers publish what they want, and the network goes out and finds it.
The website must make this concept understandable within 5 seconds.

HOW IT WORKS
Create a premium four-step process:
01 — Buyer Posts
A qualified buyer publishes the exact type of business they want to acquire.
02 — The Network Sources
Brokers, owners, advisors, and deal finders identify businesses that match the buyer’s criteria.
03 — Opportunities Are Submitted Privately
Matching businesses are submitted through BuySide with relevant financial and business information.
04 — Close & Get Rewarded
If an eligible introduction results in a completed transaction, the finder may earn a success-based reward.
